# explainroo

> Launch video. Every claim here is true of the kit as built.

## hook
This video was made by a coding agent. [#nomodel] No video model, [#nokeys] no API keys, [#noedit] and no editing. [#code] Just code.

## files
explainroo is a small open source kit you point your agent at. [#writes] The agent writes two files: [#script] a script of what to say, [#scenes] and a few lines of JavaScript for what to show.

## voice
The narration is spoken by Kokoro, [#local] a voice model that runs on your own computer. [#whisper] Then Whisper listens back and times every word.

## sync
So a picture can appear exactly when it is mentioned. [#code] Write one line, [#say] say the word database, and there it is.

## draw
The frames are drawn in a headless browser, [#looks] in one of five looks, [#sound] with music and sound effects made to fit.

## eyes
Your agent cannot watch the video, [#give] so explainroo gives it eyes: [#stills] still frames, [#sheets] contact sheets, and a check [#check] that catches cut off text, bad timing and mumbled words.

## render {min=5.5}
One command renders the finished video.

## outro {hold=2}
explainroo is free and open source. [#try] Point your agent at the repo, and ask for a video.
